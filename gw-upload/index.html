FAQ

Good to know

How does the AI stylist work?

You choose an occasion, budget and colour. An AI model picks the best match from our 15 suit guides and explains why.

Does it use my photos or personal details?

No. The stylist only receives the three choices you make. We never ask for photos, names or emails.

Can the AI get it wrong?

Sometimes. If the AI gives an answer we don't recognise, we pick the closest suit from our own guides and tell you so.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free. Some shopping links are affiliate links, which may earn us a small commission at no cost to you.

Where are my reviews stored?

Reviews are saved in your own browser only, so other visitors can't see them yet.

Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 2026
Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. Gentleman's Wardrobe is designed to collect as little data as possible.

1. Who We Are

Gentleman's Wardrobe is a free men's style platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your information.

2. Data We Collect

We collect very minimal data. Here is exactly what we store:

  • Reviews you submit — your name (optional) and review text, stored locally in your browser
  • Usage stats — number of visits and outfits generated, stored locally in your browser only
  • Your preferences — taglines and settings saved locally in your browser

We do NOT collect your name, email, phone number, location or any personal identifying information.

3. AI Stylist Requests

When you use the Ask the Stylist feature:

  • Only the occasion, budget band and colour you choose are sent to our server and then to an AI service
  • No photos, names, emails or other personal details are requested or sent
  • We do not store these requests on a server of our own

4. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to save your preferences and reviews. This data:

  • Is stored only on your device — not on any server
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Can be deleted by clearing your browser data at any time
  • Is not accessible to us or anyone else

5. Cookies

Gentleman's Wardrobe does not use tracking cookies. We do not use cookies for advertising, analytics or tracking purposes. The only browser storage we use is local storage for your preferences.

6. Third Party Services

We use the following third party services:

  • Google Fonts — loads fonts for our website. Google may collect limited data. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Groq — an AI service that picks an outfit for the Ask the Stylist feature. Only the occasion, budget band and colour you choose are sent. No personal data is shared.

7. No Advertising

Gentleman's Wardrobe does not display advertisements and does not share your data with advertising networks. We do not sell, rent or trade your data to anyone.

8. Children's Privacy

Gentleman's Wardrobe is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted data, please contact us.

9. Your Rights

Since we store data only in your browser's local storage, you have full control:

  • Clear your browser's local storage at any time to delete all data
  • You can use the website without submitting any data
  • Reviews are optional — you never have to submit one

10. Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on servers, there is no central database that can be breached. Your data stays on your device, under your control.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website evolves. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top when changes are made. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding your privacy, please contact us through the website. We take privacy seriously and will respond as soon as possible.
